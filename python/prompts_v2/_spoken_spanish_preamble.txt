SPOKEN-SPANISH QUALITY RULES (v2 — derived from the 2026-08-01 native-review
of the existing 9,100 exercises; these override anything below that conflicts).

1. WRITE LIKE SPEECH. Every respuesta must be a sentence a native
   Latin-American speaker would plausibly SAY in conversation. Before
   emitting an exercise, ask: "would a person actually say this?" If the
   scene is absurd (habitual murder, narrating one's own birth as a daily
   habit, a wedding causing the weather), discard and write another.

2. ACCENTS AND EÑE ARE MANDATORY in verbo, contexto, respuesta and pista:
   tenía, años, niños, jardín, pasábamos. ONLY the grupos arrays are
   accent-stripped (that is a speech-recognition matching artifact — it must
   NEVER leak into the display/TTS fields). "anos" is a different word than
   "años"; treat a stripped accent as an error.

3. PISTA IS IN SPANISH. It is read aloud by a Spanish TTS voice. Never
   English.

4. COMPLETE CLAUSES ONLY. No dangling connectors: never end or start a
   sentence with a bare "cuando por fin.", "mientras.", "Cada vez que,".
   If a connector appears, its clause must be complete.

5. VERB CLASS DETERMINES THE TEMPLATE (see verb_metadata.json):
   - haber: existential/auxiliary only — never a personal subject.
   - gustar-class (gustar, encantar, importar, interesar, preocupar,
     molestar, bastar, pesar, doler): dative structure "a mí me gustaba el
     mar" — conjugate for the THING, include the clitic.
   - ocurrir/suceder/pasar: an EVENT is the subject (3rd person).
   - casarse, acordarse, despedirse, enterarse, alejarse, acercarse:
     include the reflexive clitic; it gets its own grupo.
   - soler: presente and imperfecto only — it has no future or conditional.
   - Never invent verbs; "tranquilar" does not exist (tranquilizarse).

6. NATURAL WORD ORDER. "yo ya había terminado", never "ya yo había...".
   Clitics before conjugated verbs. Adverbs where speech puts them.

7. POLARITY. "nadie/nunca/nada" after the verb REQUIRE "no" (or another
   negative) before it: "no engaño a nadie". Never "yo engaño a nadie".

8. TIME EXPRESSIONS MUST MATCH THE DRILLED ASPECT and not contradict each
   other: no "en junio mañana"; no habitual marker ("cada semana") on a
   punctual preterite; anterior (pluscuamperfecto) needs a past reference
   point the action precedes.

9. MORPHOLOGY DOUBLE-CHECK before output: conjugation of the drilled form
   (nazco not naco; oiría not oíría), irregular participles (había oído,
   hubiera descubierto — never an infinitive after haber), subject–verb
   number agreement, and indicative vs subjunctive as the drill requires.

10. SELF-REVIEW PASS: after generating the batch, re-read every item
    against rules 1–9 and silently fix or replace violations before
    returning the JSON.
